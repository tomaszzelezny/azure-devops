## Download the report as an image

The hub now has a **Download report** button next to **Refresh**. It saves what you see as a single PNG, ready to drop into a status email, a retro board or a slide.

### New

- **Download report** (hub). The image has:
  - a header with the project, team, work item types, states, threshold, whether the current iteration is included, and the date and time of the data;
  - summary numbers: items in progress, median and 85th percentile age, the oldest item, items over the threshold, and items that changed state in the last 30 days;
  - the chart with its legend;
  - a per-state table (count, median and P85 age, longest time in the state) and the six items longest in their current state.
- The report uses exactly the current view: team, types, states, threshold, the current iteration filter and states hidden in the legend all apply.
- The report follows your Azure DevOps language: Polish for Polish, English for everything else, including date and number formats. State and type names stay as your process defines them. The rest of the extension stays in English.
- When nothing is left after filtering, or while data is loading, the button is disabled ("No work items to report"), so you never get an empty image.
