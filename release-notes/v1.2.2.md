## Report polish

### Changed

- **Report header** now reads in two lines: the scope on the first (project, team, threshold, current iteration) and the filters on the second (work item types, states).
- **Date of the data** moved to the bottom left of the report.

### New

- **Pick the report language** when you download. *Download report ▾* opens a small menu with English and Polski. Your Azure DevOps language is listed first.
